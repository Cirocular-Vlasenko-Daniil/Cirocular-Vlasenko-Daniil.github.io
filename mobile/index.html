How it works

  1. BuildDrag in blocks: headings, text, images, quotes, QR codes. Type sizes are already tuned for print.
  2. Download the PDFPages are laid out on the sheet in the right order — the top row is upside down, that’s intended.
  3. PrintAny printer, A4 or US Letter, 100% scale. Or take the file to a print shop.
  4. FoldThree folds and one cut — and you’re holding an eight-page zine.

How to fold a one page zine

  1. Print the PDF on a single sheet at 100% scale.
  2. Fold the sheet in half lengthwise, then unfold it.
  3. Fold it in half widthwise, then in half again — you get eight panels. Unfold.
  4. Fold it in half widthwise and cut along the fold across the middle two panels only (the dashed line on the diagram).
  5. Unfold, fold lengthwise and push the ends toward the center — the cut opens into a diamond.
  6. Flatten it into a booklet — page 1 ends up as the cover.

Why

A zine is the simplest way to put your thoughts in print: no publisher, no print run, no permission. I want creative tools to be equally accessible and easy to use for everyone. Everyone deserves a chance to put their thoughts on paper without paying for subscriptions and software licenses from megacorporations.

There should be ten minutes between an idea and a printed page — not overcomplicated design software and the fear of a blank sheet. So there are no extra settings here: just blocks, a grid and typefaces that read well on paper. Done is better than perfect.

FAQ

How do I make a zine online for free?
Open the editor — there’s nothing to install and no account to create. Add headings, text and images to ready-made blocks, download the print-ready PDF, then print, fold and cut. The whole zine takes about ten minutes.
What is a one page zine?
A tiny eight-page booklet folded from a single sheet of paper with one cut. It’s the classic format for DIY zines, comics and flyers.
What paper size do I need?
A4 or US Letter for a pocket-size zine, A3 for bigger pages. The editor lays the pages out on the sheet for you.
Can I print it at home?
Yes, any printer works. Margins leave room for printers that can’t print to the edge. Print at 100% scale.
Is it free? Do I need an account?
It’s free, with no sign-up and no ads. Your zine is stored in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
